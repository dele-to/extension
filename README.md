The official Chrome extension for [DELE.TO](https://dele.to). Encrypt selected text locally, create a secure share, and copy its link without exposing plaintext or encryption keys to the server.

## Usage

1. Select text on a page or paste a secret into the extension.
2. Choose its expiration and maximum number of views.
3. Create the encrypted share and send the copied link.

You can also share selected text from the context menu or with `Ctrl+Shift+S`  (`Command+Shift+S` on macOS). (with ability to change keyboard command in extension settings)

## Why it is safe

- Secrets are encrypted locally in your browser.
- Plaintext and encryption keys never leave your browser.
- Shares expire automatically and can be limited to a single view.

The complete share URL is sensitive: anyone who has it can open the share while it remains available.
